# Freatic monitoring-tube screening requirements

Status: design requirement, not yet an admitted algorithm.

The live BRO smoke demonstrates why TS06 availability filtering must be separated from hydrological suitability screening. Long, fully assessed GLD series can come from filters that are too deep or otherwise not representative of the freatic groundwater table required for WDM/LHM comparison.

A future screening capability should at minimum evaluate:

- screen top and bottom depth below local ground level;
- whether the filter is in or immediately below the expected groundwater fluctuation zone;
- evidence for confined, semi-confined or artesian conditions;
- groundwater-head plausibility relative to local ground level and hydrogeology;
- tube status and measurement quality;
- continuity and relevant period coverage;
- local spatial representativeness relative to the LHM observation operator;
- WDM and LHM calibration lineage.

Do not introduce a single hidden screen-depth threshold as a proxy for freatic suitability. Any threshold or decision rule must be documented, hydrologically justified, tested against known cases, and allow explicit exceptions.
